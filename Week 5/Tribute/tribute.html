1958–2016 | American Singer, Songwriter, and Music Producer

BIOGRAPHY

Prince Rogers Nelson was born June 7, 1958. He was an American singer, songwriter, and producer. He is widely recognized for his iconic music, and experimental androgynous style. He defined the Minneapolis sound, fusing funk, rock, pop, and soul.

Prince & Music

He took pride in his music quality. Despite bending genres, he was strict on what good music should be. Often criticizing over commercialized art, and surface level interaction with his music. His ideals revolve around a hands on, personal, rooted approach.

He was insanely talented, and knew how to play 27 instruments. Among those instruments, he is known for being a master guitarist. He wrote a majority of his songs, save for a couple co-written lyrics on features. As well as producing most of his own songs and sound.

The Love Symbol

The love symbol is a glyph original to Prince. It’s a combination of the male and female sex symbols. The number 7 is also included in the design, as it was an important number for Prince.

More about Love Symbol

He could no longer go by the name Prince as it was owned by the Warner. bros. So, by adopting the symbol as his name, he was fighting against corrupt music labels.

Later on Love Symbol #2 was a color created by Pantone to honor prince. The blue based deep purple is known as his official color.

LEGACY

Industry Impact

Prince was one of the first major artists to fight publicly for ownership masters. He actively tried to maintain his creative control over his art, by limiting access to his music. All of this was to go against an exploitative music industry.

"If you don't own your masters, your master owns you"

Prince wrote slave on his face as an act of rebellion (1993-1994).

Cultural Influence

He pushed boundaries of sexuality and identity. Leaving a long lasting take on aesthetics and masculinity. His visuals are iconic, recognizable and often referenced in pop culture. He redefined on what it meant to be a male artist, and what it meant to be a man in love.

Lasting Reputation

Long after his passing, he is still ranked among the greatest musicians of all time. His career and catalog has been consistently studied for songwriting, production, and arrangement.
