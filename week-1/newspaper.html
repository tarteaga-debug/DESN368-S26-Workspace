Ghosts Are Real,
But Gen Z Doesn't Care

Ghosts have always been a mystery. Whether they existed,
are here to harm us, or something worse. Now, as the internet
is part of everyday life, we finally have proof of ghosts.
So why doesn't Gen Z care?

Goodbye Cringe, Hello Stonewalling

Gen Z grew up with the tin-foiled, and stubborn boomers.
Then followed along the cringe, dramatic, and silly faced millenials.
Gen x faded between the two.

Let's just say, Gen Z has been quick to disown any identity related
to the generations before them. They hated the over enthusiastic
informercials, and embarassing attitude adopted by millenials.
Right now, disinterest is cool. Saying something is 'cool' is not.
Showing out too much enthusiasm is embarassing, because it's
just not that deep.

So, this had lead Gen Z to have a lackluster reaction to Ghosts.
Many of them simply wouldn't handle such disrespect in their
home, and they claim, they would fight a ghost over it.
Or they would just leave. Some are too tired of working to
even care about what goes bump in the night.
Safe to say they take pride in knowing they
are surviving a horror movie.
