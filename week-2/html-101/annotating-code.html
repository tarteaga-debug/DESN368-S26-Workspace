Banana Pudding Recipe

This is a recipe big enough to serve 4 or 5 people.
Or in my experience, 2 people with just enough time and cravings.
Simply double for a bigger party, and you'll even save time on measuring if you do.

Ingredients

  • Roughly 4 Bananas
  • 1 Whole Box Of Nila Cookies
  • 3.4 oz Vanilla Pudding box
  • 2 cups of milk
  • 4 oz Cream Cheese
  • 8 oz Heavy Whipping Cream
  • 7 oz Sweetened Condensed Milk
  • 1 tsp Vanilla

Instructions

Pudding Mixture

Use box for instructions
I will be using Jello cook and serve.

Cream Cheese Mixture

  1. In one bowl, begin by whipping the heavy cream until it's light and airy
  2. In a seperate bowl, whip the cream cheese as well
  3. Then, mix vanilla and sweetened condensed milk into cream cheese
  4. Fold in cream cheese mixture into the heavy cream.

Assembly

  1. Place a flat layer of cookies, make sure not to overlap.
  2. Then scoop a layer of pudding, and spread
  3. Apply a flat layer of sliced bananas
  4. Add a scoop of the cream cheese mixture, and then spread
  5. Keep repeating until you run out. Usually about three layers
  6. Any leftover cookies should be crushed, and sprinkled over the top
  7. Place in fridge to set for a minimum of 4 hours

Then you're done!
